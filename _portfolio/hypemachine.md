HypeMachine is a Chrome extension built to keep you motivated through long study sessions. Every 15 minutes, a card appears in the corner of your browser with a random personal photo and an encouraging message — then quietly disappears after 8 seconds.

Messages and images are stored locally and never committed to version control, so your personal content stays private.

**Tech Stack:** JavaScript, CSS, HTML, Chrome Extension API
