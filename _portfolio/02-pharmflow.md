PharmFlow is a web app built for pharmacy students that transforms lecture notes (PDFs) into multiple study formats: multiple-choice and true/false practice exams, spaced-repetition flashcard sets, and concise key-concept summaries.

It also includes a drug reference tool that pulls real FDA prescribing information from RxNorm and OpenFDA with no AI involvement, just actual clinical data.

**[pharmflow-ldd.vercel.app](https://pharmflow-ldd.vercel.app/)**

**Tech Stack:** Next.js 15, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, Supabase, Groq (Llama 3.3 70B), Vercel
