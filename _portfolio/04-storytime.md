StoryTime is a social TV tracking app. Log what you're watching, rate episodes and individual episodes, write reviews with optional spoiler tagging, and see what your friends think. Friend reviews are surfaced first on each show's page so the opinions that matter most are always front and center. Shows can be tracked by status: Watching, Finished, Want to Watch, or Dropped. A discovery section surfaces trending, top rated, and currently airing content.

**[story-time-silk.vercel.app](https://story-time-silk.vercel.app/)**

**Tech Stack:** Next.js 16 (App Router), React 19, Tailwind CSS v4, shadcn/ui, Supabase (PostgreSQL, auth, storage), TMDB API, Vercel
